How local SEO works in Grand Junction

Google ranks local results on three things: relevance, distance, and prominence. Relevance comes from your categories, services, and website content. Distance is where the searcher is. Prominence comes from reviews, citations, links, and how well known your business is online. A Grand Junction SEO campaign improves relevance and prominence so you show up across Grand Junction, Colorado, and the towns around it.
