The front desk never really closes

Calls, tenant questions, maintenance requests, showings, and follow-ups eat the day. For Western Slope real estate and property management teams, that work is repetitive and text-heavy. After hours it still lands on someone.

Most firms already try ChatGPT-style tools. Few have AI wired into the actual inbox, phone line, or work-order flow. The opening is implementation, not another login.

What AI can take first

A local build that answers, books, triages, and drafts, with a person on the exceptions.

After-hours tenant and leasing questions

Availability, rent, pets, parking, and "is this an emergency" get a clear answer when the lobby is closed. Staff review the ones that need a human.

Showing booking

Leasing inquiries turn into scheduled showings, even at night, instead of sitting in voicemail until morning.

Maintenance triage

Tenant requests come in by phone or text, get sorted by urgency, and land as a draft work order for your team or vendor.

Owner-update drafts

Monthly notes, occupancy, and work completed get a first draft from the data you already keep, so managers edit instead of starting from a blank email.

Built for Western Colorado property teams

This is AI for property management in Western Colorado: Grand Junction, Fruita, Palisade, Montrose, and the towns you actually cover. Residential, commercial, long-term, and short-term offices all share the same time drain. The first win is after-hours intake.

77% of US small businesses say they use AI regularly, but only 12% have ever paid for a standalone AI tool (Intuit 2026 AI Impact Report). 73% of owners say they would benefit from more training and implementation help (Goldman Sachs 10KSB, 2026).

That is the gap this work fills. You keep the tools you already use. We connect the workflow so tenants, leads, and owners get a reply without someone glued to the inbox.
