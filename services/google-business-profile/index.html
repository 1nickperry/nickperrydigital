Google Business Profile

Setup and monthly care for the listing that shows up in Google Maps and the map pack.

For a lot of local companies, the Google listing is the real homepage. It is where the phone number, the hours, the reviews, and the photos get judged in a few seconds.

Setup is included with the $1,000 website build and with both monthly plans. Monthly care covers posts, review replies, category checks, and cleanup when spam or a duplicate shows up. You keep ownership of the profile. I should never be the only person who can get into it.

What you get

  • Claim or cleanup of the existing profile
  • Categories, services, and attributes matched to the work you want
  • Photos and weekly posts on a monthly plan
  • Review request setup and replies in your voice
  • Duplicate and spam cleanup
  • A monthly note on calls, clicks, and direction requests

Google Business Profile questions

My profile was suspended. Can you help?+

Often, yes. I review what Google flagged, gather the documents they ask for, and file a reinstatement. Simple cases move in days. Messy cases take longer, and I will tell you which one yours looks like.

Do weekly posts matter?+

They are a small part of the picture, not a magic lever. They give the listing fresh offers and a link back to the right page. I include them on the monthly plans because a silent listing looks neglected.

Who owns the listing?+

You do. I will not build a profile that only I can access. If I am removed as a manager, the listing stays yours.

Is this a separate bill from the monthly plan?+

No. Profile setup and monthly management are inside GBP + Website at $850 and the AI plan at $1,500. The website build also includes setup.
