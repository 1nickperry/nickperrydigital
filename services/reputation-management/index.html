Service

Reputation management

A steady way to ask for reviews, reply in your voice, and deal with fakes without begging customers.

Reviews are part of local search, and they are also the thing a person reads in the truck before they call the next company. A pile of unread one-star posts, or a listing with no new reviews for a year, both cost you jobs.

On the monthly plans I set up a short ask that goes out after the work is done, reply to new reviews, and flag fakes. I will not buy reviews or tell customers what star to tap. That is how listings get wiped.

What you get

  • A simple review link and a message you are willing to send
  • Timing that follows a finished job, not a random Tuesday blast
  • Replies drafted in your voice for you to approve or for me to post
  • A process for unfair or fake reviews
  • Notes in the monthly report on review velocity and themes

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Set the ask

    One link, a short message, and a moment in the job when asking is reasonable.

  2. Step 2

    Reply

    New reviews get a response. Patterns in the comments tell us what the site should say more clearly.

  3. Step 3

    Protect the listing

    Duplicates, spam reviews, and impersonation get documented and challenged.

Reputation management questions

Can you remove a bad review?+

Only if it breaks Google's rules. A real customer who is unhappy usually stays up. I can help you reply and, when it is allowed, file a dispute. I will not promise a deletion.

Will you write fake reviews?+

No. I will not ask friends to pose as customers, and I will not sell review packages.

Is this included in the monthly plan?+

Yes. Review strategy and management are part of both the $850 and $1,500 plans.

What if we barely have any reviews?+

Then the first job is a clean ask after real jobs, not a gimmick. New reviews take weeks or months. That is normal.
