Service

Google Ads

Search ads when you want calls this month, quoted around your budget. Not bundled into the monthly SEO plans.

Ads are for demand you want to buy while SEO and the listing compound. I manage search campaigns for businesses that already know which jobs are worth a lead. The management fee is a custom quote based on the account and the ad budget. Google bills the ad spend itself.

This is not the same thing as Local Services Ads. Where a category qualifies, Local Services Ads setup and management sit inside the monthly plans. Standard Google Ads, the auctions with keywords and ads you write, are the add-on.

What you get

  • A recommendation on whether ads are even a fit
  • Campaign structure around services and towns, not one mixed ad group
  • Conversion tracking for calls and forms before spend scales up
  • Negative keywords and search-term reviews
  • A plain report on spend, calls, and wasted queries
  • You own the ads account

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Decide

    If the site or the listing cannot take a call cleanly, we fix that before buying more traffic.

  2. Step 2

    Launch small

    The first weeks are for learning which searches are real jobs and which are noise.

  3. Step 3

    Trim

    I cut queries that do not match the work and shift budget toward the ones that do.

Google Ads questions

Is Google Ads included in the $850 plan?+

No. Search ads management is a custom quote. Local Services Ads, where Google offers them for your category, are part of the monthly plans.

How much should I budget for ads?+

That depends on the job and the town. I will not invent a number on this page. On the strategy call we look at what a lead is worth and start from there.

Do I own the account?+

Yes. The account should be created under your Google login. I get access to manage it. If we stop, the campaigns stay with you.

Can ads replace SEO?+

They can cover a gap. They do not build the listing or the site. When the spend stops, the calls from ads stop with it.
