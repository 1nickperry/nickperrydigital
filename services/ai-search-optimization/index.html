AI search optimization

Make the business easy for answer engines to understand and cite. Included in the $1,500 monthly plan. Not a promise of a ChatGPT mention.

People are starting to ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google's AI answers for a plumber, a dentist, or a shop in a specific town. Those tools lean on the same public record as classic search: your site, your Google listing, reviews, and directories. They also like pages that answer a question in plain language.

AI search optimization is the extra work in the $1,500 plan. It includes an AI visibility check, content shaped for answers, monitoring, and local outreach. I do not guarantee that any assistant will recommend you. Anyone who does is selling a result they do not control.

What you get

  • A check of how your name, services, and towns are described in public
  • Pages and listing text that answer real customer questions
  • Alignment between the site, the Google profile, and major directories
  • Monitoring for whether AI tools mention you, with the limits of that check stated
  • Local link and press outreach, with placement fees billed by the publisher
  • A monthly note on what changed

How it works

  1. Step 1

    See the record

    I look at what an assistant can actually find: the site, the listing, reviews, and conflicting directory data.

  2. Step 2

    Make it quotable

    We tighten service descriptions and FAQs so a tool can repeat them without inventing details.

  3. Step 3

    Watch and adjust

    Each month I note mentions I can verify and the gaps that are still blocking a clear recommendation.

AI search optimization questions

What is AI search optimization?+

It is the work of making your public information clear enough that answer engines can describe you correctly. It sits on top of ordinary SEO and a healthy Google listing.

Can you get my business into ChatGPT?+

I can improve the sources those tools tend to use. I cannot force a mention. The $1,500 plan includes the work. It does not include a guaranteed placement.

How is this different from SEO?+

SEO is still the base: a clear site, a real listing, and consistent facts. AI search work adds answer-style content, a visibility check, and outreach. The $850 plan is the base. The $1,500 plan adds this layer.

Does this matter for a small town?+

Yes, if customers in that town are asking assistants for recommendations. The same cleanup that helps Google also helps those tools. A thin page with a city name stuffed into it does not.
