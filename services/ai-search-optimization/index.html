What is AI search optimization for local business?

AI search optimization (also called AEO or generative engine optimization) is the work of making your local business show up when people ask AI tools for a recommendation. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and other AI search engines pull from your website, your Google Business Profile, reviews, and trusted sources. Traditional SEO still matters, but AI search visibility adds a new layer.

How AI search works for small businesses

When a customer asks "who is the best plumber near me," AI engines look for clear, consistent facts: what you do, where you work, and what customers say. Small businesses with strong local SEO, complete profiles, and well-structured websites are easier for AI-driven search to cite.

How AI tools find local business information

AI tools draw on many sources. For local questions, those often include Google Business Profile data, business websites, review platforms, directories, maps data, and articles or lists that mention local businesses. The clearer and more consistent your information is across those sources, the easier it is for an AI engine to describe you correctly.

Step 1: make your Google Business Profile complete

Google AI features and Google Maps both rely on your profile. Complete categories, services, hours, attributes, photos, and description. Keep posting. Build reviews. A complete profile is the foundation for both local SEO and AI search visibility.

Step 2: write pages that answer questions

AI tools pull clear answers from pages that state facts plainly. For each service, answer the questions customers really ask: what it costs, how long it takes, which towns you serve, what is included, and how to get started. Use headings phrased as questions and give a direct answer in the first sentence below.

Step 3: add structured data

Schema markup is code that tells search engines exactly what a page is about. LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQPage schema help search engines and AI tools understand your name, location, services, and answers without guessing.

Step 4: keep your business information consistent

Your name, address, phone number, hours, and services should match across your website, profile, and directories. Conflicting details make AI tools less confident and can lead to wrong answers about your business.

Step 5: earn mentions from trusted local sources

AI engines often cite lists, news articles, and organization pages. Local PR, sponsorships, partner pages, and chamber listings create those mentions. Our $1,500 per month plan includes local link and PR outreach for this reason. Placement fees charged by a publisher or event are billed separately.

Step 6: build genuine reviews

Reviews give AI tools language about what customers like about you. A steady stream of detailed, honest reviews helps both your local ranking and how AI search describes you.

Step 7: monitor AI visibility

We run an AI visibility audit to see whether major AI tools mention your business for your key services and towns, then monitor changes over time. You see the results in a plain-English monthly report.
