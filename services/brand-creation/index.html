Small business branding in Colorado

Colorado businesses compete on trust. A plumber, a winery, a law office, and a landscaping company all need customers to recognize them and feel confident calling. Branding is how you create that recognition: a logo, colors, fonts, and a voice that stay consistent on your website, Google Business Profile, trucks, signs, uniforms, and social media.

What is brand identity?

Brand identity is the visible part of your brand. It includes your logo, a color palette, typography, and how photos and graphics look. A style guide documents those choices so anyone making a flyer, a sign, or a web page uses them the same way.
