Service

Brand identity and style guide

A name treatment, colors, type, and a short guide so the site and the Google listing look like the same business.

A website cannot fix a muddled name. If the truck, the Google listing, and the homepage each introduce the company a different way, people hesitate before they call.

This is a focused identity pass, not a six-month rebrand. You get a wordmark or a simple logo, a small palette, type choices, and a guide your next vendor can follow. It is a custom quote. It is not included in the $1,000 website build.

What you get

  • Wordmark or simple logo, with files you can hand to a printer
  • Color and type choices that work on a site and on a Google profile
  • A one-page style guide: clear space, do and do not, and file names
  • Guidance on the business name as it should appear in search
  • You own the files

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Listen

    We start with what customers already call you, what the truck says, and which jobs you want more of.

  2. Step 2

    Draw a tight set

    I show a small number of directions. We pick one and refine it. You are not sorting through a pile of unused concepts.

  3. Step 3

    Hand it off

    You get the files and the guide. The website and the Google listing then use the same name and the same look.

Brand identity and style guide questions

Is branding included in the $1,000 website?+

No. The website build assumes you already have a name and a basic look, or that we use a clean type treatment. A drawn logo and a style guide are quoted on their own.

Can you match a logo I already have?+

Yes. If the files are usable, I will build the site and the listing around them instead of redrawing the brand.

Do I own the logo?+

Yes. When the invoice for the identity work is paid, the logo and the guide are yours.

How long does this take?+

A tight identity pass is usually a couple of weeks, depending on how fast feedback comes back. I will put the timing in the quote.
