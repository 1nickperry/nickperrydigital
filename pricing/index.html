Small business website costs in Colorado: what to compare

When small businesses in Colorado compare website costs, the quotes often look very different. A free website or website builder plan, a freelancer, a web designer, and a full agency all price differently. Compare what each one includes, not just the number.

  • Design: template or custom design built for your services
  • SEO: whether the site is built for search engines and local search
  • Integration: booking, forms, call tracking, or ecommerce
  • Ownership: whether you own the site and domain
  • Maintenance: who handles hosting, security, and updates
