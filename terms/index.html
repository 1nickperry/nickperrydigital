Terms

These terms cover use of nickperrydigital.com and the basics of hiring Nick Perry Digital. A written quote controls if it conflicts with a general line on this page. This is a plain-language summary for a one-person practice, not a substitute for advice from your own lawyer.

The site

The pages are information about my services. Posted prices are the current offer for the standard scope described on the pricing page. They are not a bid for every possible project. Larger work is quoted before it starts.

The offer

  • A website build is $1,000. It includes an SEO-ready site, Google Business Profile setup, and 90 days of SEO after launch.
  • GBP + Website is $850 a month.
  • GBP + Website + AI Search is $1,500 a month.
  • The care plan is $150 a month and is optional.
  • Google Ads management, logo work, and websites beyond the standard build are custom quotes.
  • Monthly plans have no long-term contract. You may stop at the end of a month.
  • You own the website, the domain, and the content you paid for, once the related invoices are paid.

What is not promised

I do not promise a specific Google rank, a set number of leads, or a mention in ChatGPT or any other AI product. I do promise the tasks and the contract terms listed on the pricing page. Reports will describe work that happened, not invented statistics.

Your responsibilities

You provide accurate business details, access to accounts you want me to use, and timely feedback. You confirm that photos and text you supply are yours to use. You keep ownership of your Google Business Profile. I should not be the only person who can access it.

Acceptable use of this website

Do not misuse the contact form, attempt to break the site, or scrape it in a way that degrades service for others. Placeholder phone numbers and emails are not an invitation to send unlawful or abusive messages.

Payment

Invoices are due on the schedule in the quote. I may pause work if an invoice is overdue. Ad spend, publisher fees, and other third-party bills are yours, even when I help manage the account. Those parties bill you directly or I pass the cost through only when the quote says so.

Ending the work

Either of us can end a monthly plan at the end of the current month. I will hand back logins and site files that you own. The 90 days of SEO included with a website build run from launch unless the quote states a different start.

Limitation

I will do the work with care. I am not liable for indirect losses, lost profits, or platform decisions by Google or any AI company. If I am liable for a paid project, the limit is the amount you paid me for that project in the prior three months, except where the law does not allow a limit.

Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Colorado, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. The Western Slope is the region I serve. That is not a street-address claim.
