Privacy

This page explains what Nick Perry Digital does with information you send through nickperrydigital.com. It is written for this site, not copied from a larger company's policy. It is not a contract for client work. Project terms are covered on the terms page and in the quote you accept.

Who this is

Nick Perry Digital is Nick Perry's practice for websites and local SEO on Colorado's Western Slope. The public contact email on this site is a placeholder until the mailbox is confirmed. Until then, do not send sensitive information through the form.

What the site collects

If you use the contact form after it is connected, the form collects your name, email address, and message. A hidden field is there to catch simple spam bots. If you include a phone number in the message, that is collected too.

The host that serves the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested. Those logs are for security and troubleshooting.

When a booking link is added, the calendar is provided by that third party. Their site will receive whatever you type into their scheduler, under their own policy. Until the link is set, no calendar runs on this page.

This site does not run advertising pixels and does not sell lists of visitors.

How it is used

Messages are used to answer you and, if we work together, to scope the project. I do not sell or rent contact details. I share them only with the tools needed to reply (for example an email host or a form processor once one is connected) or if the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Form messages and email are kept long enough to answer you and to keep a record of what we agreed, then deleted when they are no longer needed for that purpose. Server logs follow the host's normal retention.

Cookies

The site itself is static and does not set a tracking cookie for ads. If a form tool or a calendar is embedded later, that provider may set its own cookies when you use their widget. The booking panel will say when a live scheduler is connected.

Children

This site is for business owners. It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can ask what I have from you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete a message that is no longer needed. Use the contact details on the contact page once they are live. If the placeholders are still in place, the request can wait until the mailbox works. You can also leave the site without sending a form.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be on this page with a new date.
