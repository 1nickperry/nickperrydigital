About

Nick Perry Digital is my practice. I build websites and handle local SEO for businesses on Colorado's Western Slope.

I put my name on the work so you know who is writing the pages, who is in the Google listing, and who answers when something breaks.

Why the Western Slope

Search here is local and practical. A person in Fruita, Montrose, Rifle, or Durango is usually looking for a business they can call today. A national playbook misses the distance between towns, the seasonal rushes, and the way a Google listing does more work than a homepage for plenty of service companies.

I focus on this region because the towns are specific. Grand Junction is not Durango, and a Redlands page is not a Palisade page.

What I do

Websites, Google Business Profile setup and care, local and organic SEO, AI search optimization, reviews, Google Ads when you want them, and a small care plan so the site does not go stale after launch. The list is on the services page. The prices are on the pricing page.

How a project runs

  1. A free strategy call. You tell me the towns you serve and the jobs you want.
  2. I send a fixed scope. The published prices cover the standard build and the two monthly plans. Larger work is quoted before it starts.
  3. I build or repair the site and the listing.
  4. On a website build, 90 days of SEO are included after launch. A monthly plan continues only if you want it.
  5. You own the site, the domain, and the content. There is no long-term contract.

What I will not put on this website

I will not invent clients, quotes, star ratings, or traffic numbers. The results page is a layout waiting for real projects. If a logo or a quote shows up later, a person agreed to it.
